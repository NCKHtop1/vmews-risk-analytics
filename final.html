VIETNAM EQUITY RISK · EARLY WARNING

Detect deterioration.
Act before losses accelerate.

VMEWS is a risk-monitoring system for Vietnamese equities. It does not predict a target price and it does not issue buy/sell calls. It identifies deteriorating risk states, explains the evidence, checks whether similar states historically preceded deep drawdowns, and translates the result into risk-control actions.

What this system is designed to answer

  1. Is market or single-name risk deteriorating?
  2. Is the warning supported by multiple independent evidence modules?
  3. What happened after comparable historical states?
  4. Should exposure, concentration, monitoring or escalation controls be reviewed?

Composite scores are risk indices, not probabilities. Historical analog event rates are empirical evidence from matched past states, not guaranteed forecasts.
