05 · RESEARCH BASIS

What comes from the thesis — and what does not.

The thesis developed an ANFIS + VAE EWM on Vietnam market data from 2007–2023, used a 3.09σ crash criterion, walk-forward retraining and reported strong classifier and stress-test results. Those figures are retained as research benchmarks only.

Operational-model disclosure

The original trained ANFIS/VAE weights, full raw research dataset and exact production hyperparameters are not contained in the thesis PDF. VMEWS therefore does not claim that its live score is the original ANFIS/VAE output. The live engine is an independently implemented, thesis-aligned and fully explainable risk system with its own out-of-sample diagnostics.
