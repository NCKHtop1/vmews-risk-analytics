RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

What the system measures

  • Which listed securities show material deterioration at date T.
  • Whether independent single-name models support the same warning.
  • For liquid HOSE names, whether a pooled market-specific model identifies elevated 20-session drawdown risk.
  • Whether out-of-sample evidence is strong enough to display a calibrated estimate.
0–100 structural scores are risk indices. The pooled HOSE calibrated crash estimate is a separate predictive-evidence layer and cannot create RED/YELLOW while its standalone alert gate is not approved.

MODEL GOVERNANCE

Research controls

  • Canonical policy VMEWS-ALERT-POLICY-4.0.0 defines WATCH 50 / YELLOW 65 / RED 78 across the market scan, detail labels, watchlist and chart.
  • Market-wide RED/YELLOW requires current EOD data, liquidity, weak trend and multiple independent stress signals; a drawdown alone cannot force RED.
  • The current model state is the latest completed feature date. It is deliberately not treated as a labelled training example; forward labels end at least 20 sessions earlier.
  • Primary event: 20-session forward drawdown ≤ -12%.
  • Chronological model-fit, probability-calibration and test windows are separated by a 20-session-equivalent purge; no random train/test split.
  • The pooled HOSE candidate is chosen on development folds only and tested on a sealed last-period block.
  • Pooled predictive promotion requires event depth, PR-AUC above base rate and structural baseline, positive Brier skill, ROC-AUC ≥ 0.58 and a date-block bootstrap PR-AUC lower bound above the base rate.
  • The pooled standalone alert gate is separate. If that gate fails, pooled probability/rank may be shown as evidence but cannot create RED/YELLOW or autonomous action.
  • Raw model/anomaly scores are not automatically probabilities. User-facing probability estimates are calibrated separately.
  • VNINDEX evidence is excluded rather than imputed neutral when unavailable.
  • Raw prices remain visible on the investor chart. Large one-day discontinuities are neutralized only inside research returns/labels and disclosed as corporate-action suspects.
  • Current news/fundamental/macro context is separated from historically validated price-volume models unless dated vintages are available.
MODEL SET

Independent research components

  • Whole-market T-day structural deterioration screen.
  • Deterministic structural EWS and historical analogs.
  • Single-name regime clustering, seeded Random Forest and adaptive neuro-fuzzy inference.
  • Seeded VAE anomaly model and seeded LSTM crash/rebound classifiers.
  • Pooled HOSE champion: market-specific cross-sectional classifier trained over tens of thousands of point-in-time panel states; model complexity is selected by development evidence rather than by preference for deeper AI.
  • Full-universe headline NLP with relevance, source quality, event taxonomy, deduplication and time decay.
  • Chronological calibration, validation-aware weighting, model ablation and sealed pooled promotion.
