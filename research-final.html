MODEL GOVERNANCE

Research controls

  • Canonical policy VMEWS-ALERT-POLICY-4.0.0 defines the 0–100 WATCH/YELLOW/RED bands used by the market scan, deep-research index and chart.
  • Market-wide RED/YELLOW requires current EOD data, liquidity, weak trend and multiple independent stress signals; a drawdown alone cannot force RED.
  • The current deep-model state is the latest completed feature date. It is deliberately not treated as a labelled training example; forward labels end at least 20 sessions earlier.
  • Primary deep-research event: 20-session forward drawdown ≤ -12%; rebound event: 20-session forward gain ≥ +12%.
  • Chronological model-fit, calibration and test windows are separated by a 20-session-equivalent purge; no random train/test split.
  • Supervised model outputs and anomaly/regime scores are mapped through chronological isotonic calibration before they contribute to the calibrated event estimate.
  • PR-AUC, Brier skill, event depth and model ablation govern reliability weights; weak models can receive zero effective weight.
  • The 0–100 deep risk index is a historical risk percentile with a limited current-context overlay. It is not the calibrated event probability.
  • VNINDEX uses an independently refreshed EOD context snapshot; unavailable benchmark evidence is excluded rather than imputed as neutral.
  • Raw prices remain visible on the investor chart. One-day discontinuities above the corporate-action research guard are neutralized only inside model returns/labels and are disclosed as suspects.
  • Current news/fundamental/macro context is separated from historically validated price-volume models unless dated vintages are available.
