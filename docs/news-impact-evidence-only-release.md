## Hybrid V2 Research Infrastructure (03 Nov 2026)
- NLP: existing deterministic_semantic_v1 and NEWS-EXPOSURE-RESEARCH-1. LLM is OFF until independent precision/recall and provider/credential/latency audit; never claim active foundation-model inference.
- Economic Exposure: new reviewed issuer filing schema enforces an actual publication timestamp, disclosure period, effective date, material exposure share and coherent mechanism. Financial statements without segment/export/country/FX-debt disclosures are counted as coverage, not affected-ticker evidence.
- Shadow evidence: current news snapshots generate a separate versioned research snapshot. Only research counts and missingness are shown in verified-only UI; no issuer-specific confirmed badge.
- Prospective ledger: captures clustered stories whose ENTIRE story membership has an independently measured collector receipt, never newspaper publication time. An immutable first-seen record is retained for later out-of-sample tests. Legacy syndicated records cannot create valid PIT evidence.
- Quant: frozen V12 abnormal-return correlation remains retrospective, no causal proof. Prospectively dated disclosure facts + independent matched-control outcomes, placebo, sample stability, FDR and independent review are required before proposing any event-to-issuer/industry relation.
- Production kill switch: zero verified relations, zero impact notifications. Data-only edits cannot override these protections.
- UX: no noisy popups; a quiet research-coverage paragraph, neutral empty state, and date freshness check. Do not assign the News priority score as impact strength.
