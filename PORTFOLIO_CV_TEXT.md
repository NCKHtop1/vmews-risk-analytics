## Short CV version
**Vietnam Market Early Warning System (VMEWS) — Market Risk Analytics Portfolio**  
Built a deployable VN-Index risk monitoring platform inspired by an ANFIS + VAE stock-crash early-warning thesis. Implemented a serverless market-data pipeline, data validation, time-series risk features, 0–100 live risk monitoring, backtest/stress-test visualisation, sector analytics, and reproducible Python reference models. Clearly separated research benchmark results from live implementation assumptions to support model governance and auditability.  
**Tech:** Python, Pandas, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, JavaScript, Chart.js, Vercel, Time Series, Market Risk, Model Risk.

## LinkedIn / portfolio version
I converted a Vietnamese stock-crash Early Warning Model research framework into a deployable analytics product. The research layer uses ANFIS and VAE concepts, while the live layer ingests VN-Index market data, performs validation and feature engineering, and displays current volatility, drawdown, momentum, anomaly and risk state. I also added benchmark comparison, stress-testing results, sector statistics, data lineage, source freshness and a clear reproducibility disclosure so the dashboard does not claim unsupported model accuracy.

## Interview one-liner
“I treated it as a model-governance project, not just a dashboard: research claims are frozen, current data is controlled, and any new live score is clearly versioned and explained.”
